# Product image sources

These are **stock photographs of each kind of sweet**, not photographs of
Nakhye's Ashok Sweets' own products. They are locally optimized derivatives of
images published on Wikimedia Commons, cropped to a square and resized to
800×800 so the card's `object-fit: cover` never crops the subject away.
Replace them with the shop's own photography before launch.

34 photographs cover 168 of the 195 products; the
remaining 27 render `_placeholder.svg` (see below).

Every file was opened and looked at before it was mapped. Candidates were
rejected for showing a competitor's brand or price card, a watermark, a
caption burnt into the image, food mid-preparation, or a different sweet than
the name suggests.

## Why 27 products have no photograph

A wrong photograph is worse than an honest blank, so where no Commons image
actually shows the product on sale, the product renders `_placeholder.svg`:

- **Bangali** (1) — Cham Cham. The two candidates were a chomchom in a plastic bag and a shot that included a rival shop's price card.
- **Khara** (1) — Bundi Tikhat. The one boondi photograph is the sweet slab, and this product is the savoury one.
- **Ladu** (7) — Dink, Haliv, Methi and Rukhwat laddu, none of which are photographed on Commons. Rava, besan, til and motichoor laddu do have their own pictures.
- **Pedha** (10) — all Modak. The shop sells *mava* modak; every modak on Commons is the steamed or fried dumpling, which is a different sweet.
- **Vadi** (8) — Aalepak, Amba, Batata, Dudhi, Gajar, Kohalla, Shrikhand and Tilwadi vadi. Only naral vadi is photographed.

Dropping a correctly-named JPEG into this directory and repointing
`src/catalogue.json` is all that is needed to fill one of these in. Bump
`PRODUCTS_KEY` in `src/main.tsx` at the same time, or returning browsers will
keep serving themselves the catalogue they cached.
